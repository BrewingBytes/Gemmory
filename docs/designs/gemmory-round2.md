## Problem Statement
Gemmory 0.5.0 (versionCode 12, on origin/main) has every feature testers and the founder asked for (photo canvases, about 48 bundled pictures, Today's picture, remote pictures, streak and gift wheel, inventory, placement tools, milestones, celebration, achievements). It still has no pull: nothing tells a player to come back, the supply of new pictures depends on the founder by hand (0 remote pictures published), and the founder finds the gems ugly and the sounds thin. Founder: "we need smth to pull people." This round picks the features that bring players back the next evening, give them something new to come back to, and make the evening session feel good enough to repeat. Each feature becomes a GitHub issue (T35+) for implementers.

## Demand Evidence
- No tester evidence yet. Founder: "nothing specific yet, we implemented all testers and I wanted." Every pick in this round is a hypothesis, to be measured in early production (see Success Criteria).
- Category evidence (web, directional): the return hooks in color-by-number apps are a daily new picture and themed collections (Happy Color: a Daily section with a yearly calendar, seasonal collections such as "Winter Whispers" and "Best of 2025"; ColorPlanet sells "new pages every 24 hours"). Vendor data: push click-through tracks D1/D7/D30 in game apps; 57% of Android game players churn on install day.
- Code evidence: `androidApp/src/main` has no notification code at all, so Today's picture and the streak work only for players who open the app on their own.
- Supply evidence: about 48 bundled pictures, 42 of them locked. A daily player meets a new Today's picture for about 6 weeks, then it runs dry. `pictures/v1/` has 0 published pictures.

## Status Quo
Between evenings nothing reaches the player. A relaxer who doesn't think of Gemmory goes back to whatever coloring app reminds her, or to nothing. The founder's only content lever is a manual `publishRemotePicture` run plus a PR per picture.

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge
- **The evening relaxer** (the founder's clearest tester profile): plays on the couch in the evening to unwind, mostly bundled pictures, finishes Quick and Medium canvases, rarely shares. Photo makers stay important for installs through shares, but they aren't this round's target.
- **Narrowest wedge:** one opt-in evening notification that announces something real (a picture that became available tonight, Today's picture, a streak about to break), backed by a schedule of pictures that appear on their own. If that doesn't move next-evening return, nothing else in this round will.

## Constraints
Unchanged from round 1: photos never leave the phone and there is no backend for user content; no ads on the play canvas; adults only; `shared/src/commonMain` stays pure Kotlin; a wrong-color tap changes nothing; UI follows `docs/design-system.md` unless the founder approves a change (the gem redesign is that approval, given in this session).
New for this round: notifications are local only (no server, no FCM).

## Premises
1. Build for the evening relaxer. A feature counts if it brings her back the next evening (D1/D7), not by shares.
2. Feel is first-class but ships in **wave 2** (revised after the second opinion): the gem redesign, placing sounds and ambient music are issues, ranked behind the pull features. The gem redesign keeps no-gap tiling and the picture's exact colors (recognizable photos is still risk #1).
3. Notifications are local, opt-in, at most 1 a day at the player's evening time, and only about something real.
4. (Revised) A small content-supply fix (scheduled drops, collections) AND new game mechanics are both in scope.
5. Installs this round come from the store page and ratings (in-app review prompt), not from relaxers sharing. The share loop stays as is.

## Cross-Model Perspective
Codex was unavailable (`gpt-6.1-sol` isn't supported with a ChatGPT login). A fresh Claude subagent did the cold read:
- **Steelman:** a nightly ritual: something new waiting each evening, one gentle reminder at the right time, and placing that feels good enough to become a habit, all on the device, with no server.
- **Key quote:** "nothing specific yet, we implemented all testers and I wanted, but we need smth to pull people." Everything asked for has been built and there's still no pull, so the gap is a reason to open the app tomorrow. The pipeline for new pictures exists, and nobody is feeding it.
- **Challenged premise 2:** gems, sounds and music lengthen sessions but don't create a return. 12 testers can't produce a real D1/D7 signal, so this round is picked on taste until production. Founder outcome: premise 2 revised, feel moved to wave 2.
- **48-hour build:** `availableFrom` in the remote catalog plus 14 pictures as one themed set; one opt-in evening notification (asked after the first finish, default 20:30, priority new drop > Today's picture > streak, skipped if already opened that evening, deep link); notification and session events; a sampled placing sound. It would skip the gem redesign, music, new mechanics and the in-app review (reviews stay private on a closed track).

**Wave 1: pull**
1. **Evening reminder (T35).**
   - **Permission and prompt:**
     - An in-app sheet ("Remind me in the evening?" with Yes / Not now) shows after the player's first finished picture, never at first launch.
     - **Yes:** on Android 13+ the system `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` dialog follows. On Android 12 and below there is no runtime permission, so Yes alone turns reminders on. Reminders are on once granted.
     - **Not now:** the sheet asks once more after the 3rd finished picture, then never again.
     - **A system-dialog denial:** the sheet is never shown again.
     - **Notification channel:** "Evening reminder", default importance.
   - **Settings:** a Reminders toggle and a time picker.
     - If notifications are blocked by the system or the channel is off, the toggle shows off with "Notifications are off for Gemmory" and an "Open settings" button to the app's system notification settings.
     - Turning the toggle on when permission is missing asks for it (Android 13+) or opens system settings, whichever Android allows.
   - **Delivery:** a once-a-day background job at the time the player picked (default 20:30 local time).
     - It uses inexact scheduling with no exact-alarm permission. Android gives no firm delivery bound: Doze, App Standby buckets and OEM battery savers can delay it by hours.
     - **Mechanism** (eng E8): `AlarmManager.setAndAllowWhileIdle(RTC_WAKEUP)` at the chosen local time, handled by a receiver with `goAsync` (whole run ≤ 10 s). It fires during Doze and is usually delivered within about an hour on Android 12+; there is no delivery guarantee, and the late-run rule drops anything later. No new dependency.
     - **Background starts** (eng O1): the receivers are enabled only while reminders are on. A process started by a receiver doesn't count as a cold start for the first-session ad rule (`AdPolicy.onProcessStart` moves to the first foreground), and billing, ads, Play Games and the achievements backfill wait for the first foreground.
     - **Time range:** the Settings picker offers 17:00 to 22:00 in 15-minute steps.
     - **Late-run rule:** the job computes every reason for its scheduled local date. If it runs more than 2 hours after the chosen time (so never past midnight), or on a later date, it posts nothing and logs `reminder_late` (minutes late).
     - It is rescheduled after every run, on boot, app update, a clock or time-zone change, any Settings change, and every app start (a force-stop clears alarms).
     - The reason is computed inside the job from on-phone state (catalog, grids, Today's picture, streak, saves, play files). Before that, when the catalog check is due, the job starts or joins the gallery's guarded check (catalog plus today's grid only), cancelled after 8 s; a timeout or offline falls through (eng E4, outside voice #8).
     - **Reads** (eng): the job runs in the app process and reads through `CanvasStore`, whose single writer dispatcher serializes it with canvas writes; files are atomic renames. The job never writes a save. Unstarted remote pictures are summarized from catalog metadata, not decoded (eng O2).
     - **New play-time state** (outside voice #4, required): saves don't record when a gem was last placed or when a picture was finished (`CanvasRecord.kt:9` holds title, createdAt, playMillis). T35 adds that state: last gem time per canvas and a finish date per picture, used by the skip rule, "almost finished", the streak tap target and T38's window. **Storage** (eng E1): a new "GMRT" v1 `play` file in each canvas directory (last gem millis, first finished local day), written on the writer thread with the save and on finish. Start over resets the last gem and keeps the first finished day. No migration, and photo canvases stay out of backup with their directory. Pictures finished before T35 have no date: they count for collections, not for events.
     - **Today's picture from the job** (eng E2): the job makes the day's pick through the same synchronized `TodaysPictures.pick` the gallery uses, with candidates from one shared function moved out of `GalleryViewModel.todayCard`, so the notification and the Today card always name the same picture.
   - **What it says, by priority** (the first that applies wins):
     1. **Tonight's drop:** a T36 picture whose `availableFrom` is today, already stored and verified on the phone, and not started. If it is also Today's picture, the drop copy is used.
     2. **Today's picture:** not yet started.
     3. **Streak at risk:** the streak is 2 days or more, no picture has been finished today, and it really breaks at midnight (`DailyStreak.shown(state, today + 1) == 0`, so not when a forgiven day is left; outside voice #7).
     4. **Almost finished** (CEO E4): an unfinished canvas past 50%, played in the last 7 days; if several qualify, the most recently played: "You're 82% through Lighthouse". A photo canvas is only ever "your photo canvas", with no title and no image.
     5. **None:** no notification goes out.
   - **Skip rule:** skipped while the app is in the foreground, or if the player placed a gem in the 3 hours before the job runs that day. A skip suppresses reasons 1, 2 and 4; reason 3 can still apply (below). Opening the app without placing a gem doesn't count. Exception: reason 3 is still sent while no picture has been finished today, because placing gems doesn't save the streak.
   - **Tapping it:**
     - **Drop or Today's picture:** opens that picture's Preview.
     - **Streak:** opens the most recently played unfinished canvas, or the gallery if there is none.
     - **Almost finished:** opens that canvas, or the gallery if it's gone.
     - **Stale tap** (tapped after midnight, the picture withdrawn, or no longer free to start): opens the gallery, with no error.
   - **Large icon:** the picture's flat preview, for a drop, Today's picture or an almost-finished bundled or remote picture. The app icon for a photo canvas (never its image) and for a streak reminder. An unfinished mystery picture uses the frost (design review D23).
   - **Copy names the picture** ("Tonight: Lighthouse at Dusk"). A photo canvas is "your photo canvas"; an unfinished mystery picture is "tonight's mystery picture" (D23).
   - **Time picker** previews the copy: "We'll remind you around 20:30."
   - **Measurement** (CEO O1): no holdout. Reminders go to every player who opts in. T35 is judged on open rate and before/after D1/D7 (Success Criteria).
   - **Events:**
     - `reminder_prompt_shown` (attempt = 1 | 2).
     - `reminder_prompt_result` (result = granted | declined_in_app | denied_system).
     - `reminder_settings_changed` (on, minutes since midnight).
     - `reminder_shown` (reason = drop | today | streak | almost_finished), logged only when a notification is actually posted.
     - `reminder_late` (minutes late).
     - `reminder_opened` (reason).
     - `canvas_started` gains `entry` (see T36-T38).
   - **Copy:** calm, following the design system, with no urgency language beyond the true streak fact. The founder approved the ask and notification copy table, shown in full on the lock screen (design review D21). The ask body names every reason, "a streak to keep" included, and the design system's streak entry allows the streak fact in this opt-in note only (D10). After a Yes, one gallery line "Reminders on, around 20:30. Change it in Settings." (D12). A return that completes a collection shows no ask (D7).
2. **Scheduled drops (T36).**
   - **Catalog:** the remote catalog gains an optional `availableFrom` (local date). A picture is hidden until that date. On the date it shows with the New label and is preferred as Today's picture.
     - For a dated picture, `isNew` and `todayCandidates` count from `availableFrom`, not from the fetch day. Today `firstSeen` is the fetch day (`RemoteState.kt:33-34,50`), so a prefetched drop would never be New on its date.
     - Drops default to `locked = true` in the T44 CSV, since Today's picture for free players only picks locked pictures.
   - **Lapsed players** (accepted limit): the catalog is fetched only while the app is in use, so a lapsed player has only the drops prefetched before her last session. The founder keeps the published calendar at least 21 days ahead. The reminder's bounded catalog check (T35, eng E4) also reaches lapsed players.
   - **Publishing:** `publishRemotePicture` takes an `availableFrom` per picture. The per-row date in T44's CSV is the single source of truth, with no cadence parameter. The founder signs a month of pictures in one PR.
   - **Prefetch:** when the catalog is fetched, the phone downloads and verifies the grids of future-dated pictures (sha256 + crc32, as today) and keeps them hidden until their date. A drop that isn't stored and verified by its date stays hidden until it is. The reminder never names it.
   - **Cadence** (CEO C4): one drop every date. T44 warns when fewer than 14 days are scheduled ahead.
   - **Access** (CEO C2, eng E3/E9): a drop is free to start on its `availableFrom` local date only. Its first gem unlocks it for good. After its date it follows its catalog `locked` flag (video, token or VIP). Today's drop is preferred over other New pictures as Today's picture; if it arrives after the day's pick, it's free beside that pick, and the Today card shows the drop.
   - **Hidden means hidden** (eng, outside voice #5): `RemotePictures.listed()` (or the one wrapper every caller uses) leaves out future-dated and unearned pictures, so a crafted or stale intent can't open them.
   - **Gallery refresh** (eng): the gallery reloads at local midnight while it shows and after a check that brings something new.
   - **Tomorrow teaser** (CEO E3; design consultation D5 = T2): tomorrow's prefetched drop shows as a line at the bottom of the Today card (its own card when there's no Today card), with a frost of the picture, "Tomorrow" and "A new picture opens at midnight", no name and no countdown. Tapping it shows the "Opens tomorrow" toast (design review D8). A drop opens at local midnight of its date, so the copy never says "evening". Event: `teaser_tapped`.
   - **"New today" chip** on the Today card when today's picture is a drop. This is new UI, so `docs/design-system.md` gets it.
   - **Integrity:** the `issuedAt` anti-rollback and the signature rules stay as they are. A moved clock can reveal a picture early, which is an accepted risk (the same as Today's picture). Future drops and mystery art also sit in the public `pictures/v1/` folder weeks early, another accepted risk: the C7 rule holds inside the app only.
   - **Events:** `canvas_started` / `canvas_finished` gain:
     - `entry` = gallery | today_card | reminder | collection | event.
     - `days_since_available`, for drops.
     - `day_played` (entry), once per local day on the first gem placed, with no ids (eng O3): the denominator for the drop and event criteria.
3. **Themed collections (T37).**
   - **Catalog** (eng E6): a top-level `collections` list in the signed catalog: `{id, title, members: [picture ids], event?: true}`. Members may be remote or bundled ids; picture entries carry no collection field and stay frozen once published. Collection and event ids share one namespace, separate from picture ids.
   - **Gallery** (design consultation D6 = R1; design review D3-D6, D19, D25): at most two rows above the grid (the event in its window first, then the newest incomplete collection; one row above 1.5× font; none before the player's first finished picture), each with its name, "3 of 7 finished" and a strip of its pictures, then an "All collections" text button that opens a sheet with every row. States, large-screen and 200% font rules are in `docs/design-system.md`.
   - **Membership:** each collection's members are fixed in the catalog, with a declared size, and may mix bundled and remote pictures.
     - Members not yet dropped count in the size and show as "2 more coming", with no picture.
     - A withdrawn member that was never started leaves the collection and its size. A started one stays.
     - A device that hasn't fetched the catalog shows no collection rows (none could complete without it).
   - **Access:** members keep their own access rules (bundled lock, drop rules). Finishing counts no matter how a picture was opened, so a locked member can be opened with a video, a token or VIP, as today.
   - **Reward** (CEO C6): finishing every member earns both:
     - a collection-only picture: a signed catalog entry with `rewardOf` = the collection id, hidden until earned. T37 defines `rewardOf` and the hidden-until-earned rule; T38 reuses them;
     - 1 unlock token (`inventory_earned` source = collection).

     VIP has no inventory, so VIP gets the picture only. An earned reward picture is unlocked for good and shows with the New label. A keepsake once earned is kept, even if the collection or the reward picture is later withdrawn: a reward picture that was earned stays, like a started remote picture. **Mechanism** (eng, outside voice #2): on earn, its grid is copied into its canvas directory through the existing create path, and the earned id goes into `PictureUnlocks` (backed up and transferred); an earned picture shows even after the catalog withdraws it.
   - **Fetched catalog only:** completion and the reward are decided only against a fetched, verified catalog. A phone with only the bundled members never shows a collection as complete.
   - **Completion glint:** a small `lamp` glint on the collection row the moment it completes (reduced motion: none). The return that completes a collection has no interstitial, ask or review, and the glint plays once the row is on screen (design review D7). Goes into `docs/design-system.md`.
   - **Events:** `canvas_started` / `canvas_finished` gain `collection_id`; `collection_completed` (collection_id). These are content ids, an explicit exception to `Analytics.kt:15`'s "no ids" rule. The privacy policy and Data safety text say so.
   - **Constraint:** no new currency.
4. **Weekly event (T38).** A themed week built from drops and collections: a collection of 7 pictures that drop on 7 consecutive dates.
   - **Window:** from the first drop's `availableFrom` through the 6th day after it, inclusive, in the phone's local dates.
   - **Reward:** finishing 3 of the 7 within the window unlocks that week's exclusive picture for good. Finishing after the window doesn't count.
   - **The exclusive picture:**
     - It is an ordinary signed catalog entry with `rewardOf` = the event's id. It is prefetched with the drops and hidden until earned.
     - VIP earns it the same way: 3 finishes in the window (CEO C3). It stays hidden until earned, so a VIP never sees a locked tile and the paywall's "Every gallery picture unlocked" (design consultation D7 = P2) stays true.
     - It's the event's keepsake: an event collection doesn't also grant the T37 keepsake picture. Finishing all 7 of an event still grants C6's 1 unlock token (not VIP).
     - The catalog marks a collection as an event with `event: true` on the collection. Collection and event ids share one namespace (eng E6), and `event_id` is the collection id.
   - **After the window:** the 7 pictures stay in the gallery and the collection under the normal drop and lock rules. An exclusive that wasn't earned stays hidden.
   - **Events:** `canvas_*` gain `event_id`; `event_reward_unlocked` (event_id, finished_in_window).
   - **Constraints:** no timers on the canvas and no ads tied to it. A week missed is just gone, with no penalty and no nag.
5. **In-app review prompt (T39)** (a launch item beside #74, not a pull feature; CEO C1). The Play In-App Review API after a finished picture.
   - **Gate:** a single AAB is promoted from closed to production, so a build flag can't separate the tracks. The prompt is switched on by a flag in the signed remote catalog (`features.review`), which the founder flips once production goes live. No app release is needed. *(Removed by T52, review prompt always on: no switch from 0.8.4 (20).)*
   - **Attempts:** the app can't tell whether Play's quota showed the dialog, so it calls at most 3 times per install, kept as a counter. Attempt *n* (thresholds 3, 10, 25) is made at the first finished picture that meets all of these:
     - the finish count is at or past the *n*th threshold;
     - `features.review` is on (removed by T52, review prompt always on);
     - it is at least 14 days after the previous attempt;
     - it isn't the day of the player's first finish.

     A deferred attempt waits for the next eligible finish. A player already past a threshold when the flag is flipped gets that attempt at their next eligible finish, one attempt at a time.
   - **Event:** `review_requested` (attempt).
6. **Picture pipeline (T44, CEO E1).** Wave 1a, beside T36.
   - **Prompt:** a checked-in ChatGPT image prompt (`tools/remote/prompts/`) that asks for a 2×3 sheet of 6 pictures in one theme, in a calm palette, sized for the converter.
   - **Splitter:** finds the 6 tiles in one sheet, trims the gutters and saves each as a source image.
   - **Batch:** a folder + CSV → converter freeze → the content-check gate → provenance rows → `publishRemotePicture` with dates. One PR per month.
     - In 1a the CSV has title, size, colors, availableFrom and locked; it needs T36's `availableFrom`. Reward rows (`rewardOf`, from T37) carry no `availableFrom` and aren't counted in the scheduled-ahead checks.
     - Each later column ships with the issue that defines its field: `rewardOf` and a collections sheet (id, title, members) with T37, the `event` flag with T38, `mystery` with T43. For a dated picture, `addedAt` = `availableFrom`.
   - **Aspect:** one aspect per sheet (1:1, 3:4 or 4:3), so the CSV is grouped by aspect; each size names its aspect (e.g. `big-1:1`). The splitter crops each tile to it. A 6-tile split leaves about 500 px per tile, far above the grid sizes (`GridSpec`), so resolution isn't a constraint.
   - **Content check:** the gate lists every picture in the batch and needs a yes for each (`-PcontentChecked=yes` today is per picture, README.md:142-146).
   - **Provenance:** AI pictures record "AI-generated", the model, the prompt file and the date. The content check still refuses real people, brands and copyrighted characters.
   - **Old-client guard** (CEO S1-1): T44 stamps each picture's `minAppVersion` with the lowest versionCode that understands every hiding field it uses (`availableFrom`, `rewardOf`, `mystery`). Collections are a top-level key that old clients ignore, so they never raise a picture's version (eng E6). A publish test fails if one is too low. A 0.5.0 phone never sees a future drop, an unearned reward or mystery art.
   - **Calendar warning:** T44 warns when fewer than 14 days are scheduled ahead (C4). A scheduled CI check on `pictures/v1/catalog.json` also fails when fewer than 7 days remain (only once the catalog has any `availableFrom`), so a founder who stops publishing still gets told.

   - **Must keep:** no-gap tiling, the picture's exact colors, the 16dp placement floor, the number legibility rules and the selected-color highlight modes.

8. **Placing sounds (T41).**
   - **Sound:** sampled or carefully synthesized gem sounds, with 3-5 variations per event on top of the random pitch, and a softer, layered tick when dragging.
   - **Events covered:** a gem placed, a color done, a batch, a milestone, the finish.
   - **Licensing:** sources must be CC0 or made in-house, with provenance recorded like the pictures.

9. **Ambient music (T42).**
   - **Content:** 2-4 calm CC0 or commissioned loops, playing in the gallery and on the canvas.
   - **Settings:** its own Music toggle and volume, separate from Sound. **On by default at 30% volume** (CEO C5); the first music plays at the first canvas open, never on the cold-opened gallery, with a one-time "Music is playing · Turn off" chip on the next gallery visit (design review D24, D20 for TalkBack); it stays silent while the ringer is silent or vibrate (eng: `AudioManager.ringerMode` checked before starting, paused on `RINGER_MODE_CHANGED_ACTION`).
   - **Audio behaviour:** ducks or pauses when other audio is playing (audio focus), pauses during interstitial and rewarded ads and the time-lapse export, stops in the background, and is never part of the time-lapse audio.
10. **Mystery picture (T43).**
   - **What it is** (design review D9, D23: founder, "all hidden" until complete): a picture variant that stays fully hidden until it's finished. Title, art on every surface (the frost), the canvas's unplaced-cell tints (one neutral tint), Peek and Share progress are all hidden; it's shared only from the completion screen, and the finish celebration is the reveal.
   - **Which pictures** (CEO C7): drops flagged `mystery` in the catalog (set in the T44 CSV), never a player toggle.
   - **Until finished, everywhere:** the frost and a neutral name ("Today's mystery picture" in the app on its date, "Tonight's mystery picture" in the reminder, "Mystery picture" after its date; D11) in the teaser, Today card, chip, collection rows, Preview, reminder icon and copy, and TalkBack.
   - **Events:** `canvas_started` gains `mystery`.

## Success Criteria
Measured in the first 30 days of production; closed-test numbers are informational (about 12 people).
- **Decides T35** (CEO O1): open rate at least 8% of `reminder_shown` with a `reminder_opened`, and Firebase D1/D7 for installs after the T35 build not below installs before it. Reminders are switched off if the open rate stays under 3% after 30 days of production. Before/after is correlational: other changes ship too. Overall D1 ≥ 35% and D7 ≥ 12% (round 1 targets, unchanged).
- Reminder opt-in: at least 40% of `reminder_prompt_shown` end in `reminder_prompt_result` = granted.
- Drops: at least 30% of daily players (`day_played` that date, eng O3) start that date's drop on its date (`days_since_available` = 0, the free window under C2).
- Collections: at least 10% of players who started a collection member complete it within 30 days (`collection_completed`).
- Supply (T44): the scheduled calendar never drops below 7 days ahead (CI check).
- Weekly event: at least 15% of players active during an event week (`day_played` within the event window, eng O3) get `event_reward_unlocked` with `finished_in_window`.
- Store rating ≥ 4.3 after 50 ratings.
- Feel (wave 2): no regression in photo recognizability (founder plus 3 testers, before/after on the converter goldens' canvases); session length tracked as informational.
