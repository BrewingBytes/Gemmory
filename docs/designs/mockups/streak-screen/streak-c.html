Streak C · home-screen widget

Fills the gaps in ui.html v3 section 5 (#103, G17): the widget at launcher size, flame tiers, 200% font, the picker preview and the TalkBack label. Fixed felt ground, no dynamic color, mono number, flat flame (still, no flicker on a home screen). Never a picture or a photo.

1 · On the home screen (2×2, Pixel launcher size)

Dark wallpaper
Not done today: grey ember flame, "Not yet today". Tap anywhere → the streak screen with the gallery under it.
Light wallpaper
Done today: the flame lit in its tier color, "Done today". The felt tile reads the same on any wallpaper.

2 · States and tiers

Number = days in a row as the chip shows it (freezes and day off counted). 0 and "Start a streak" when never played, broken, or in the repair window (the repair lives on the streak screen, not on the widget).

3 · Smallest cell and 200% font

Some launchers give a 2×2 only 110dp: the number drops to 32 and the padding to 10. The number is sized in dp, so it never outgrows the tile; the line follows font scale and wraps to at most two lines.
