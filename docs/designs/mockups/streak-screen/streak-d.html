Streak D · milestone share card

Fills the gaps in ui.html v3 section 5 (#104, G10, G14). The card is a 1080×1920 PNG, drawn here at 1/4 scale (270×480), so every size is ×4 in the PNG: flame 440px, number Atkinson Mono 700 at 384px, line Bricolage 600 at 104px, "Gemmory" 64px. It sits on felt-deep with the app's 9% grain. Each day of the milestone week, Monday to Sunday (the locale's week, as on the celebration), shows the bundled or remote picture finished that day. A day with only a photo canvas finished, or a day kept by the day off, a freeze or a repair, shows the lit flame. A photo canvas, its pixels or its colors never appear. Days after the milestone are empty outlines. The only way in is the milestone celebration's "Share" (founder, 2026-10-22).

1 · Milestone cards

The pictures here are stand-in pixel art. In the app each one is the picture's saved finished thumbnail, which only exists for bundled and remote pictures; a photo canvas's thumbnail is never read. The flame and the number take the tier color: amber under 30, orange from 30, red-orange from 100.

3 · How it's shared

Milestone celebration
From #101: Continue is the primary button, and "Share" is the text button on 7, 30, 50, 100 and 365 (G10). Other days keep "See your streak", or the repair line. The card is made on the phone when Share is tapped (well under a second). A second tap in that time does nothing.
Android share sheet
The system sheet with the PNG and the share text "Made with Gemmory: <Play link>" (the link is tagged utm_medium=streak, utm_campaign=streak_card). Back, or a share, returns to the celebration; Continue then goes on as before (G6). Picking an app logs streak_share_completed with the milestone. The time-lapse's share_completed and Show and Tell are not counted.
