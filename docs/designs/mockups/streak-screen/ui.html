Streak v3 · every decision applied

Final mockup for the Duolingo-style streak (design review G1-G18, eng E1-E8 / O1-O11). Every screen follows an approved decision; the full copy, state, accessibility and motion tables are in docs/designs/streak-screen.md. Thumbnails are stand-in pixel art; phones are 360dp.

Applied: literal flame, animated like Duolingo (grey until today's picture is done, lit after) · nudge = a line in the Today card that opens the canvas · the evening reminder puts the streak first on a night it really ends · during the 2-day repair window the chip shows a grey flame and "0" and the nudge offers the repair · after the celebration, at most one of reminder ask → Play review → interstitial, run when the gallery next shows (never between the celebration and the streak screen) · copy: true facts and weekday dates, no countdowns, no "!", no guilt · streak-hero number: mono 88sp (celebration, share card), 56sp (streak screen), capped at 64sp above 1.5× font · a killed app shows the celebration if the finish was under 30 min ago, else the chip's flame ignites · milestones: Continue primary, Share as text · gift: a line only · week row Monday-Sunday · awaiting-cover day is dashed · share card shows bundled pictures only · Milestones off = silent celebration · calendar days aren't tappable · widget 2×2 on felt.

1 · The flame

Flat vector, no glow. It flickers in a 1.6s loop wherever it's 40dp or bigger (static on the chip, in lists and under reduced motion). Color says how far along you are: amber from day 1, orange from 30, red-orange from 100.

2 · Day-extended celebration

Full screen on felt-deep-dark after the completion screen, on the first finish of a new day. The flame ignites (grey → lit, 800ms), the number rolls up (600ms), then the week fills. replay

Day 1
Copy: "You started a streak" / "Finish a picture tomorrow to make it 2." On the first finish the reminder ask follows on the gallery. TalkBack: polite "Streak started. 1 day."
Day 5 · gift line
Copy: "5 days in a row" / "Finish one tomorrow to make it 6." Gift line: "Your 5-day gift spin is in the bag." No third button; the bag shows its dot as today. VIP: no line.
Kept by the day off or a freeze
Copy: "Your day off on Tuesday kept it going." / "A streak freeze kept it going on Tuesday. 1 left." / "Your VIP freeze kept it going." The covered day fills first in its own mark, then today's flame.
Milestone 7
7, 30, 50, 100, 365: bigger flame and a 2dp lamp edge that brightens and fades once. "A week of gems" (30 "A month of gems", 50 "50 days of gems", 100 "100 days of gems", 365 "A year of gems"). Reduced motion: flame lit and still, number shown, no edge; sound and haptic stay.

3 · The streak screen

From the chip, "See your streak", or the broken nudge. Order: flame + number + best, today's status, safety (day off, freezes), next gift, the month. The week row is Monday-Sunday.

Alive, not done today
The flame is grey until today's finish. Status: "Finish a picture today to keep it" with a quiet "Today's picture" (opens the canvas). Yesterday was missed and will be covered: dashed cell and "Yesterday is covered by your day off when you finish today."
Alive, done today
Flame lit and flickering. Status: "Done for today". Calendar: each finished day shows the first picture finished that day; days kept before round 2 show a small lit flame; day off = bone outline; freeze = patina square; today = lamp outline. Cells are not tappable.
Broken, repair window (free)
Grey flame, "0", "Best: 12". Repair first: "Bring back your 12 days" / "Your streak ended on Tuesday. Watch a short video to restore it." / "Watch a video" / "You can do this until Thursday, once a month." VIP: "Bring it back". Offline: "You're offline. Connect to watch a video." in fault.
Never started / window closed
Grey flame, "0", "Finish any picture to start a streak." and a quiet "Today's picture". Best shows only if there was one. The calendar still shows finished days.

4 · Gallery and the evening reminder

Not done today chosen: N1
Chip: grey flame + "6" until today's finish (lit after, nudge gone). Line in the Today card: "Finish a picture today to keep your 6 days" / "Today's picture is Quick" → opens Lighthouse. TalkBack chip: "6 days in a row, not done today, button, opens your streak".
Target is another canvas
When Today's picture isn't Quick or is done, the line names the canvas closest to done: "Harbour is 82% done" → opens it. With no Today card, the same line is its own felt-deep card in that slot.
Broken, repair window
Chip: grey flame + "0 ›". Line: "Your 12 days can still be restored" → streak screen. After the window both go away.
Evening reminder chosen: streak first
Gemmory · 20:30
Your 6-day streak ends at midnight
Finish any picture tonight to keep it. Lighthouse is Quick.
Only on a night it really ends (day off and freezes counted). Ranks first, ahead of a drop and Today's picture. Tap → the nudge's canvas. Large icon: the app icon (never a photo).

5 · Freeze, widget, share card

Inventory sheet
New 4th row "Streak freezes" / "Covers a missed day after your day off. Hold up to 2." The wheel's freeze slice reads "Freeze" (at 2 held: "5 fills").
Widget 2×2
Fixed felt ground (no dynamic color). Not done today: grey flame "Not yet today"; done: lit "Done today"; broken/new: "Start a streak". Tap → streak screen with the gallery under it.
Milestone share card
The tier flame, the number, the milestone line, then the milestone week Monday-Sunday: each day the bundled or remote picture finished that day; a photo-only day or a kept day shows the lit flame (never a photo canvas or its colors); days after the milestone are empty outlines. 1080×1920 PNG.
