Five slices clockwise from the top, sized by their odds: 5 fills 45, 10 fills 20, photo canvas 15, unlock token 10, streak freeze 10. While 2 freezes are held, the freeze slice reads and pays "5 fills" (E7). The odds are in words under the wheel; TalkBack reads "Gift wheel. 5 fills 45%, …, Streak freeze 10%".

Before spinning

Landed on the freeze
"A streak freeze added to your inventory." in the polite live region; then a quiet "Done".
At the cap (2 held)
The slice's label and the odds line read "5 fills" (the cross stays, so the wheel keeps its look); landing pays 5 fills: "5 fills added to your inventory."

First section of Your streak while an offer stands (broken streak of 2+ days, within 2 days of the day it ended; a video repair any number of times). The flame stays grey and the number reads 0 until it's restored.

4 · Gallery nudge (PR 3)

For a streak of 2+ days with nothing finished today. A line under a hairline in the Today card that opens its canvas directly. The target is Today's picture if it's Quick and unfinished, else the canvas closest to done, else a Quick picture. It's gone once a picture is finished today. With the kill-switch flag off it never shows.

Today's picture is Quick
"Finish a picture today to keep your 6 days" / "Today's picture is Quick" → Lighthouse's canvas. TalkBack: one node, "Finish a picture today to keep your 6 days. Today's picture is Quick, button".
Closest to done
Today's picture is Big (or done): "Harbour is 82% done" → Harbour.
A Quick picture, yesterday awaiting cover
Nothing started: "Beach Huts is Quick". Yesterday was missed and the day off covers it on a finish: muted extra line "Yesterday is covered when you finish".
No Today card, Tomorrow line too
Every picture is open (VIP has none, or today's is done and the card is gone): the nudge is its own felt-deep card in that slot. The Tomorrow line (T36) sits under it in the same card.
Broken, repair window

Font above 1.5x
The line wraps; it replaces the 2nd collection row, so the grid doesn't move further down.

Only on a night the streak really ends at midnight (day off, VIP freeze and freezes held all counted: with a freeze in the bag there is no streak reminder). On that night it outranks tonight's drop and Today's picture. Tap → the nudge's canvas. Large icon: the app icon (never a photo).
