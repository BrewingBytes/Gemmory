## Problem Statement
Gemmory 0.8.1 (versionCode 17) is on the Play closed test, tagged v0.8.1 (Test Lab passed 2026-10-22), with every round-1 and round-2 feature and streak A-D built. The 14-day closed-test clock is running; production access comes after it. There is no production data, and nobody but the founder really plays: the testers are friends and family who run the clock. Production will be the first time a stranger finds and opens Gemmory. The founder's worst day-30 outcome is "Nobody installs". This round picks the work that gets strangers to the store page, through it, and through the first session without help, and makes the first 30 days of data readable. Each pick becomes a GitHub issue (T45+).

## Demand Evidence
- None from strangers. The founder on who plays the closed test: "Mostly me". On watching a stranger's fresh install without help: "Never".
- Round 1 planned the evidence and it didn't happen: before/after posts in r/diamondpainting and a Facebook group, and 25 recruited testers (`docs/designs/gemmory.md`, The Assignment). The closed test was filled from friends and family instead.
- Category (web, vendor case studies, directional): localized store listings raised organic installs in agency case studies (one reported +55% in 60 days); organic game growth in 2025-26 comes from short, low-polish, native-looking 9:16 gameplay clips (one title went from 10 s to under 7 s clips and its daily installs rose sharply), and a clip only compounds with an in-game share path.
- Code facts: the app and its store listing are English only. `androidApp/src/main/res` has `values` and `values-v31`, 3 string resources; user-facing copy is hard-coded in about 48 app files and built in about 19 `commonMain` files (e.g. `ReminderCopy`, `CollectionCopy`, `StreakText`). The share link (`timelapse/ShareLink.kt`) carries `utm_source=share`, `utm_medium` (bundled | photo | streak) and `utm_campaign` (timelapse | streak_card), but no picture, and the app reads no install referrer itself.

## Status Quo
Players find diamond-painting apps through Play search, which is dominated by large titles and ranked per language. A German or Brazilian player searching in their language can't find Gemmory. A time-lapse shared to a feed loses the share text, so the Play link never travels. On first launch a stranger may meet the EEA consent form, then Google's own "Create a Play Games profile" sheet (Play Games' automatic sign-in at app start, `README.md` → Play Games setup), before the starter picture.

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge
- **The stranger on day one of production:** has never heard of Gemmory, found it through a search in their own language or a clip, and gives it one session. Rounds 1-2 built for the player who stays; this round builds for the player who decides in five minutes.
- **The founder as first creator:** posts clips of daily drops and finished pictures to TikTok, Reels and Shorts before and after launch.
- **Narrowest wedge:** a first session with nothing between the install and the first gem, plus a funnel that can be read on day 30. If those fail, nothing else this round matters.

## Constraints
Unchanged: adults only; user photos never leave the phone (no upload, no backend for user content, out of backup); no ads on the play canvas; `shared/src/commonMain` is pure Kotlin; a wrong-color tap changes nothing; UI follows `docs/design-system.md` unless the founder approves a change.
New for this round:
- No production access yet; pre-registration, custom store listings and listing experiments are locked or need traffic until production. The work must land on the closed track first and keep working when the same AAB is promoted.
- No backend: install attribution and the shared picture ride on Play's install referrer only.
- A shared link never carries anything about a photo canvas (no id, no title, no image).

## Approaches Considered
### Approach A: Launch kit (minimal viable)
First run clear, funnel check, founder clip tool, machine-translated listing (founder checklist). Effort S, risk low. Not chosen alone: the translated listing opens an English app.
### Approach B: Launch kit + a localized app (ideal architecture)
A, plus every user-facing string moved into resources (with a pure-Kotlin copy layer for `commonMain`), the app and listing in ~5 languages, and the gated feed variant. Effort L (human ~3-4 weeks / CC ~1-2 days), risk medium.
### Approach C: The picture travels with the link (lateral)
A, plus a shared time-lapse's Play link carries the picture's id in the install referrer, so the new player's first open lands on that picture. Effort M, risk medium.

> **Amended by the CEO review (Phase 2) below.** Where the two differ, the CEO section wins: in particular T45's simpler Play Games rule (O2), T46's fresh-install rule and `first_launch` denominator (O5, O6), T49's eleven languages, fonts, titles and tooling (C3, C11, X1, X2, X4), T47's stills (X3), the new T52, the two waves (C1) and cloud save deferred (C5).

1. **First run clear (T45).** A fresh install goes consent (EEA, as today) → gallery → starter with nothing else in front.
   - Play Games' automatic sign-in and its "Create a Play Games profile" sheet wait until the gallery shows in a later process than the one where the player first finished a picture (a process start that opens a canvas first, from process-death restore, a reminder, the nudge or the widget, waits for the gallery), never at first launch, never over a canvas and never on the return from a finish. Accepted: Android may keep one process alive for days, so for some players this comes late; that's fine for a feature with no install or first-session effect. The return from a fresh finish already has its one interruption (celebration first on a new streak day, then at most one of reminder ask · review · interstitial; design system navigation map, #101 G6), and a stranger's first finish is also a new streak day and the reminder ask's slot, so Play Games can't share it. On that later start Play Games takes the gallery's one interruption; the music chip (T42), if still due, waits for the next gallery visit.
   - **Mechanism:** a backed-up flag `playGamesReady` (set when the first fresh finish is counted; set at once on upgrade for a player with any finished canvas or an earned achievement in the ledger, so existing players see no change; an upgrader with neither, signed in today only through the provider, loses Play Games until their first finish, accepted). `PlayGamesBackend` doesn't call `PlayGamesSdk.initialize` until the flag is set, the process is a later one, and the gallery's one-interruption arbiter (the code that already picks ask · review · interstitial) grants Play Games the gallery's turn. Until then every Play Games call is a no-op, the Settings "Achievements · Play Games" row is hidden (as today while Play Games is off), and achievements earned meanwhile wait in the ledger (`AchievementLedger`); the backfill runs at the first sign-in.
   - The SDK's `PlayGamesInitProvider` is removed from release builds too (`docs/todos.md`, Platform; debug already drops it, #113), so `PlayGamesSdk.initialize` runs only from `PlayGamesBackend`. Verified on a release-signed closed-test build with a tester account (the todo's condition): a fresh install shows no Play Games sheet before the first finish, and after the next start sign-in and an unlock work.
   - Audit everything else that can show before the first gem on a fresh install (consent, reminder ask, music chip, streak, collections, review, interstitial) and write the order into the design system's navigation map. Anything found stacking is fixed in this ticket.
2. **Launch funnel (T46).** The founder can read day 30.
   - Verify in Firebase DebugView, on a fresh install of a release-like build, that `first_open` → `canvas_started` → `day_played` → `canvas_finished` → `share_tapped` → `share_completed` all fire with the documented params.
   - New `first_gem` event (`ms_since_first_open`, `source`, `entry`), so time-to-first-gem has a number. The first-open time is stored on the phone at the first launch of the T46 build. A launch is a **fresh install** only when `PackageInfo.firstInstallTime == lastUpdateTime` and no backed-up state was restored (no canvas, ledger or settings on disk); `first_gem` is logged once and the referrer is read only for a fresh install, so upgraders and restored phones never pollute them.
   - Read Play's install referrer once at the first launch of a fresh install (Play Install Referrer library, bounded at ~2 s, off the main thread) and log `install_referrer` (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_content) through the usual consent gate, so share installs are counted by the app itself, not only by Firebase's own attribution. The raw referrer is kept on the phone only until T50 has used it. T50 reuses the read.
   - **Testing before production:** a debug switch `-Pgemmory.fakeReferrer=<utm string>` feeds the same parser; the real path is checked once on the closed track by installing from a crafted Play link on a tester phone with the app removed.
   - A new doc page, "Reading the first 30 days": every success criterion of rounds 1-3, the event or Play Console report it comes from, and the consented-only caveat.
3. **Founder clip tool (T47).** A "Clips" screen reached from a row that exists only in debug builds' Settings. It lists every catalog picture whose grid is on the phone (bundled, plus remote ones the catalog check already fetched: future drops are prefetched and verified, so the debug screen bypasses only the date and earn gates, never the signature) and renders its time-lapse from a synthetic placement log through the existing encoder and `FramePlan`, saved to `getExternalFilesDir(Environment.DIRECTORY_MOVIES)/clips/<id>-<order>.mp4` (`adb pull` reaches it). The watermark and app name stay on: these clips advertise the app. The log order is one of three, built in `commonMain` with tests (`ClipOrders`): `byColor` (palette slot order, cells in reading order within a slot), `ripple` (rings outward from the grid's center, 4-connected distance) and `random` (seeded shuffle). Each is a permutation of every cell index, so the log is a valid save-format log. Never in a release build, never for a photo canvas.
4. **Strings to resources (T48).** No language change: English stays word for word.
   - Every user-facing string in `androidApp` moves into `res/values/strings.xml` (plurals as `<plurals>`), including notifications and their channel names, the widget's `RemoteViews`/Glance copy (resolved with the widget's context), TalkBack descriptions, toasts, and the time-lapse and streak-card text drawn into frames.
   - Copy built in `commonMain` (`ReminderCopy`, `CollectionCopy`, `StreakText`, `A11yText`, `ShareLink.text` and the rest) returns a message key enum with typed arguments instead of English text; `androidApp` maps each key to a resource. `commonMain` stays pure Kotlin. A JVM test fails when any key has no resource or a resource's argument count doesn't match. (Compose Multiplatform resources are an alternative the eng review weighs; the app has no iOS target yet.)
   - Not ours, left as is: the UMP consent form, AdMob and Unity ads, the Play Billing and Play review sheets, Play Games' screens, the system share sheet (all follow the phone's language on their own).
   - Number, percent and date formatting follow the locale.
   - Regression contract: `StoreScreenshots` and `ScreenSizesWalkthrough` are captured on main before and on the branch after, in English, with the test clock and seeds fixed (Today card, streak and Tomorrow line depend on the date), and pixel-diffed; any difference fails the ticket unless explained.
5. **Localized app and listing (T49).** The top ~5 non-English markets (picked at the CEO review from Play's market data for the category), each with: app strings, notification copy, the store listing (title, short and full description), store screenshots made by `StoreScreenshots` in that locale, and a pass at 200% font with the longest strings. Translation source and review (machine + a native speaker, or a paid service) is the CEO review's call. Picture titles stay English unless the CEO review adds per-language titles to the catalog (a `minAppVersion` change). **Unsized until the CEO review** picks the languages, the translators and the titles rule.
6. **The picture travels with the link (T50).** A time-lapse or progress share of a bundled or remote picture adds the picture's id to the referrer: `ShareLink.url` appends `%26utm_content%3D<id>`, encoded once inside `referrer` like the other params (ids are already URL-safe). A photo canvas share and the streak card never carry an id: their link lands on the starter, with the photo flow one tap away.
   - At the first launch of a fresh install, T46's read hands over the id. If the id is a picture this phone can list (`openableRef`, so a hidden drop, an unearned keepsake, an unknown or crafted id opens nothing), the shared picture **replaces the starter as the first canvas**: after consent (EEA), the gallery shows and its canvas opens once, free to start like Today's picture (its first gem unlocks it for good). The starter keeps its first tile.
   - Accepted: anyone can craft a link with a real locked picture's id, so a fresh install can start one locked picture free, once. That's the same gift as Today's picture and costs nothing.
   - A remote id not on the phone yet (the first catalog check still running) is kept for that session and tried again once when that check finishes; then it's dropped. Nothing waits on the network before the gallery shows.
   - Only shares that keep the text carry the link (chat apps, messages, email); TikTok, Reels and Shorts uploads drop it, so T50 serves direct shares, and its success line is informational.
   - The referrer read is local and needs no consent; it's named in the privacy policy and Data safety ("install referrer, read on the phone"), and its events wait for consent like every event.
   - Events: `canvas_started` `entry` = share_link; `install_referrer` from T46. Navigation map: a new first-launch branch "share link with a listable id ─► its canvas (entry share_link)".
7. **Feed-tuned share variant (T51, gated).** Opens only if the founder's posting test shows clips getting views but few listing visits. A ≤7 s cut whose first frame is already striking (the finished picture, or the photo turning to gems for a photo canvas), with the app name and "on Google Play" burned into the frames. Same encoder, no new save or grid format. A player-facing "Share to feeds" choice vs a second file is decided if it opens.

**Candidate for the CEO review: cloud save through Play Games Saved Games.** Android Auto Backup already restores non-photo progress, unlocks, inventory and the streak on a new phone; Saved Games adds live sync between two devices and a restore when backup is off or stale. It never includes photo canvases. Needs merge rules that can't duplicate inventory or streak days, Saved Games turned on in the Play Console, and privacy policy and Data safety updates. No install effect.

- First session: ≥ 80% of `first_open` have `first_gem`; median `ms_since_first_open` under 60 s; ≥ 50% finish their first canvas (the starter, or the shared picture for a T50 install) on day 0.
