# Design: Ad spots (completion bonus, daily gift chest, "double it", rewarded interstitial)

Generated by /office-hours on 2026-11-26

Mode: Startup

## Problem Statement
Gemmory turned real ads on in 1.0.5 (AdMob + Unity bidding) and earned €0.18 on the first day. Adding bidders
(AppLovin, Liftoff, Mintegral, Pangle) failed at sign-up, so the lever left is how many ads a player chooses to
watch. Today rewarded ads appear only when the player needs something (a locked picture, a photo canvas, colors,
fills, a streak repair), and the one interstitial is capped so hard (after the 2nd finish, not in the first session,
10 min apart, 4 a day, completion only) that a typical player sees 0-1 a day.

## Demand Evidence
- Real, measured behavior: about 44 weekly active consented players (GA, 2026-11-26), 23 organic installs in 28
  days, 66 installs from the first day of a Google Ads app campaign at 0.78 RON each.
- First ad revenue: €0.02 by midday, €0.18 by the evening of the first day of ads (AdMob).
- EU consent rate 69% (17 consent messages shown), now with the message in 32 languages.
- No player-level evidence yet on how often rewarded offers are taken; 1.0.5/1.0.6 events (`rewarded_offered`,
  `rewarded_watched`, `interstitial_shown`, `ad_impression` with value) will give it within a week.

## Status Quo
Players earn fills, canvases and colors by watching a rewarded video only at a moment they are blocked; finishing a
picture, the happiest moment, pays nothing and offers nothing. The gift wheel spins only as a 5-day streak gift.

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge
The adult player who finishes a picture in an evening and comes back for the next one. Narrowest wedge: one opt-in
"Watch to get 3 fills" on the completion screen.

## Constraints
- AGENTS.md: adults only; no ads on the play canvas; interstitial-type ads only after leaving the completion screen,
  under the design doc caps; UI follows docs/design-system.md.
- Every new spot is opt-in (the player taps to watch); nothing plays on its own except the existing capped
  interstitial, which this design turns into a rewarded interstitial with a skippable intro (Google policy).
- Tiers (docs/designs/gemmory.md tier table, #38): **VIP sees none of the four spots** (hidden, not granted
  free; VIP has no inventory, no wheel, no rewarded ads). **Remove-ads players see the three opt-in rewarded spots**
  (completion_bonus, daily_chest, double_wheel) like Free players, because Remove ads removes only the
  interstitial; they **never see the rewarded interstitial**, which takes the interstitial's place.
- **Rule change for the founder to approve:** a tap-started rewarded video is allowed on a **fresh** completion
  screen (completion_bonus). The offer is disabled while a time-lapse encode or the share flow is running, and the
  video never starts during an encode; Done, Share and the time-lapse stay exactly as they are. This departs from
  today's posture (nothing ad-related on the completion screen; streak repair "never on or before the completion
  screen"), so docs/design-system.md and the gemmory.md Ad rules are updated with it.
- iOS mirrors Android later (memory: iOS copies Android behavior).

## Premises (agreed 2026-11-26)
1. The capped interstitial shows about 0-1 times a player a day, so replacing it changes revenue little; it is
   mostly a player-experience change.
2. Most of the money is in rewarded "moments of need", and the best new spot is a multiplier at a moment the player
   already values.
3. A week of 1.0.5/1.0.6 data comes before caps are locked.
4. The player promise stays: no ads on the canvas, nothing forced during play, adults only, every new spot opt-in.

## Approaches Considered
### Approach A: Completion bonus (minimal)
An opt-in "Watch to get 3 fills" next to Done/Share on the completion screen of a picture just finished.
### Approach B: Full rewarded set
A, plus "double it" on earned rewards (gift-wheel prize, grace fills), plus the interstitial replaced by a rewarded
interstitial (skippable intro, reward = fills) under reworked caps.
### Approach C: Daily gift chest
A chest in the gallery, once a day: "Watch to open" for a random prize from the wheel's table.

## Recommended Approach
**All three in 1.0.7 (founder decision, against the recommended staging of A + C first, B a week later).**
- Why together: one release and one review, with each spot measurable on its own two ways:
  - **Counts:** a new `placement` param on `rewarded_offered`, `rewarded_watched` and `rewarded_unavailable`, with
    values for the new spots (`completion_bonus`, `daily_chest`, `double_wheel`, `rewarded_interstitial`) and for
    today's (`locked_picture`, `photo_canvas`, `colors`, `inventory_fills`, `streak_repair`). It goes into the
    gemmory.md events list and is registered as a GA custom dimension **before** 1.0.7 ships (registration doesn't
    apply backwards).
  - **Money:** `ad_impression` is logged by the Mobile Ads SDK itself and can't carry our param, so each new spot
    gets **its own AdMob ad unit** (three rewarded units plus one rewarded interstitial unit), each added to the
    mediation groups, and revenue per spot is read by `ad_unit_name`. The app never logs `ad_impression` itself.
- Accepted risk: if retention drops after 1.0.7, the release alone won't say which spot caused it.
- Spots and placements:
  - `completion_bonus`: Approach A, on the fresh completion screen only (not a completion still reopened later),
    **once per canvas id ever**, so Play again or Start over can't farm fills on a Quick picture. The button
    appears only when a rewarded video is already loaded; if the video then fails, the Ad rules' "No video right
    now" line shows and **no grace is given** (grace stays with the blocked-moment offers, and the existing fills
    grace is not shared with this spot). A watch here blocks both the interstitial and the rewarded interstitial on leaving this
    completion screen, however long ago it was watched: a new per-completion flag passed to AdPolicy's exit check
    (a rule of its own, separate from the 2-min quiet after a rewarded ad).
  - `daily_chest`: Approach C, once a local day, in the gallery.
  - `double_wheel`: "double it" after a gift-wheel spin (rare: every 5 streak days). Per slice: fills are doubled;
    a photo canvas or an unlock token gives one more; a streak freeze gives a second freeze up to the cap of 2,
    then 5 fills (the slice's existing rule). The spin's own prize is added before any video, as today, so a
    killed app can't re-roll; the double is added **only after a watched video**, with an idempotent use id. The
    offer shows once per spin, right after landing, and is lost when the sheet closes; with several waiting spins,
    each gets its own offer.
  - `rewarded_interstitial`: replaces today's interstitial at the same moment, in the same "one interruption per
    return to the gallery" order (after the reminder ask and the review prompt), and under **today's caps in
    1.0.7**. Caps accounting: the intro being shown counts as an interstitial toward the 10-min gap and the
    4-a-day cap (skipped or watched); a watched video also starts the 2-min rewarded quiet. The intro appears only
    when the rewarded interstitial is already loaded and never waits on "Loading video..."; otherwise it is
    skipped silently, with no message and no grace. **Unity doesn't bid on this format** (the Android adapter
    supports banner, interstitial, rewarded and native only), so when no rewarded interstitial is loaded, today's
    Unity-bid interstitial shows instead under the same caps, keeping fill. Remove-ads and VIP players see neither.
    AdPolicy commonTest rows to add: intro counts toward gap and daily cap; watched rewarded interstitial starts the
    rewarded quiet; a completion_bonus watch blocks the interstitial on that exit; Remove ads blocks both formats.
- Unchanged: the 2-min quiet after any rewarded ad, the first-session and before-2nd-finish rules, no interstitial-type ads for Remove ads, no ads for VIP, no ads on the canvas.
- Wireframe: none (the UI is specified in the idea-to-pr design-review phase against docs/design-system.md).

## Open Questions
- Reward sizes: 3 fills for the completion bonus and the rewarded interstitial; the chest's prize table (reuse the
  wheel's odds, or a smaller table)? Decide in the eng/design review with the week of data.
- Does the rewarded interstitial keep the 4-a-day cap, or follow rewarded rules (no cap) since it is skippable?
- Where in the gallery the chest sits without competing with Today's card (design review).
- Does "double it" also apply to grace rewards, or only to the wheel?

## Success Criteria
Under the floor-of-100 rule (docs/reading-first-30-days.md): every rate is reported with its N, and no rate counts
until its cohort has 100 consented players. Until then the readout is counts.
- Per spot (readable at small N): offers shown and videos watched (`placement`), and revenue per watch (AdMob, by
  ad unit).
- Ad revenue per daily active player at least 30% above the last full week before 1.0.7, read on organic players
  only: paid-campaign installs (Google Ads, `first_user_source` google-ads) are reported separately, and no new
  campaign starts inside the comparison window, so the player mix stays comparable.
- Day-7 retention no more than 2 points below the 1.0.6 cohort, once both cohorts reach 100.

## Distribution Plan
Existing path: PR into `main`, release AAB uploaded to Internal, promoted to Open, Closed (Alpha) and Production,
the same as 1.0.6; Test Lab on the `v1.0.7` tag.

## Dependencies
- A week of 1.0.5/1.0.6 data (through about 2026-12-03) to set reward sizes and to judge later cap changes.
- Target dates: build and review 2026-12-03 to 12-09, 1.0.7 in Production at 100% around 2026-12-10.
- Four new AdMob ad units (completion_bonus, daily_chest and double_wheel as rewarded with Unity bidding; one
  rewarded interstitial, AdMob demand only) and the `placement` custom dimension registered in GA before release.
- Design doc Ad rules (docs/designs/gemmory.md) and docs/designs/ad-mediation.md updated with the new spots.

## The Assignment
By 2026-12-03, watch 3 people who are not you finish a picture in Gemmory, without helping, and write down for each
one whether they would tap "Watch to get 3 fills" on the completion screen, and what they said.

## Kill or Success Line
Read date: 14 days after 1.0.7's Production rollout reaches 100% (about 2026-12-24 if that is 12-10), with the
day-7 retention cohort being installs from the first 7 of those days.

SUCCESS — By the read date, organic ad revenue per daily active player is at least 30% above the last full week
before 1.0.7, and day-7 retention is no more than 2 points lower (once both cohorts reach 100).

KILL/CONTINUE — The founder decides on the read date: if day-7 retention is more than 2 points lower at N of at
least 100, or organic revenue per daily player is not higher, 1.0.8 first removes the rewarded interstitial
(returning today's interstitial), then the spot with the lowest revenue per watch. Below 100 players, the counts
alone decide only to keep going.

### R2-1 — feasibility (blocking)

**Problem**

> The design says a completion_bonus watch suppresses the rewarded interstitial when the player leaves that completion screen, and that 'the existing 2-min quiet after a rewarded ad already does this'. It lists the AdPolicy row 'a completion_bonus watch blocks the interstitial on that exit'. The Ad rules (docs/designs/gemmory.md:243, 'Never within 2 minutes of a rewarded ad') only block an interstitial inside 2 minutes of the watch. A player who watches the bonus and then stays on the completion screen longer than 2 minutes (making the time-lapse, sharing, looking at the result) gets the rewarded interstitial (or the fallback interstitial) on leaving. So the stated mechanism doesn't deliver the promised behavior, and the listed test row would fail against it.

**Remedy**

> Add an explicit rule to AdPolicy: a completion_bonus watched on this completion screen blocks the interstitial and the rewarded interstitial on that exit, however long ago the watch was (for example a per-completion flag passed to the exit check). Keep the 2-minute quiet as a separate rule, and change the test row to cover a watch more than 2 minutes before exit.

- Rollback: without D2, a broken spot needs 1.0.8 (build + Play review, 1-3 days). With D2, archive its ad unit
  in AdMob and the spot disappears within the ad expiry window; the rewarded interstitial falls back to today's
  interstitial the same way.

### Decisions (founder answers, 2026-11-27)
- **DR-D3 First finish:** completion_bonus only from the 2nd finished picture on (keeps D8; never stacks with T56).
- **DR-D4 Bonus look:** a centered underlined text button (GemTextButton, bone, 48dp target) with the 12dp play mark,
  16dp under Done. Order: picture, "Finished", meta line, Share (primary), Done (quiet), offer. Its slot is laid out
  from the first frame (as T56); the offer is decided once when Share/Done fade in (5.05s); if no video is ready then,
  it isn't shown that visit and the slot keeps its height. Never inserted later. Still, VIP, claimed canvas: no slot.
- **DR-D5 Daily gift home (founder's own answer):** a popup (sheet) the first time the app is opened each day;
  dismissing it doesn't show it again that day.
- **DR-D6 Gate:** the daily gift appears only after the player's first finished picture.
- **DR-D7 After dismiss:** a quiet "Today's gift" row in the Inventory sheet until claimed or local midnight, shown
  only when its video is ready. No second popup, no new bag badge.
- **DR-D8 Popup timing:** on the day's first gallery visit, once its video is ready, only while the gallery is idle
  (no sheet, dialog or picture open, no touch in the last second). If that doesn't happen on that visit, it tries on
  the next gallery visit that day; at most once a day. Never over the canvas, completion screen, Share, or another
  sheet; a return that already took its one interruption doesn't also get the popup.
