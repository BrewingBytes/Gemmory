Gemmory is on the Play closed test; production access is not open yet. ## Status Quo
- Players find diamond-painting apps through Play search, ranked per language and dominated by large titles. Competitor listings all lead with "color by number", relaxation/ASMR and themed packs; none of those seen lead with "your own photo".
- Hobbyists already pay for physical "custom diamond painting from your photo" kits. Hypothesis (unverified against Play search volume): the photo angle is an under-used search and community hook.
- Channels today, per the founder: "Mixed / unsure". Community posts brought the testers; the clip posting test (round 3, T51 gate) was "a hit" by the founder's call on 2026-10-24, with no per-clip numbers recorded (C1).

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge
- The stranger on production day one (round 3's user, unchanged): never heard of Gemmory, finds it through a community post, a clip, a friend's share or a search in their own language, and gives it one session.
- Wedge: open production, then know where each install came from and whether photo players finish. Nothing new for the player to learn.

## Constraints
- Solo founder, no paid UA. Console, store-asset and posting work is the founder's; code tickets go through the usual PR + CI `build` check.
- Photos never leave the phone; no new backend. Any new event or question goes through the existing consent gate, privacy policy and Data safety (as T46's C9 gate).
- T46's floor of 100 consented players before a rate counts: closed-test numbers are counts, not rates.
- AdMob only links apps that are public on Play: real ad earnings start at production (#74).

**#74 (T34): Real ads + Unity mediation**: unchanged scope; ships with or right after T54.

**T55: Per-channel links and a funnel readout**
- One tagged store link per channel (each subreddit, each Facebook group, each clip bio), built from the utm fields `ReferrerParse` already reads; listed in a doc page next to `docs/reading-first-30-days.md`, with the rule for naming a new channel.
- **Reserved values:** the app acts on some referrer values, so channel links never use them: `utm_source=share` (share attribution, T50/T53), any `utm_content` (T50 reads it as a catalog picture id from any source, `ShareLanding.kt:68`; channel links give up landing on a picture by choice, outside voice #14), and `utm_medium` (share links use it for bundled/photo/streak, `ShareLink.kt`). Channel links carry `utm_source` (the platform, e.g. `reddit`) and `utm_campaign` (the community or post, e.g. `r-diamondpainting-1024`), and nothing else. A referrer whose `utm_content` names no catalog picture opens nothing (gallery as usual); T55 adds a test for that if T50's tests don't already cover it.
- A readout the founder runs (Play Console's store-listing acquisition report by UTM campaign, which counts players who declined consent, plus a saved Firebase exploration or a short documented query for the funnel; decided in eng review; outside voice #3). Google signals stays off, or GA4 thresholding hides small rows (#16) that prints, by first-user source and by photo vs bundled: installs, `first_gem`, first `canvas_finished`, a day-2 `day_played`, shares. Counts under the floor of 100, rates above it.
- **Rewarded answers (C3):** T56 answers are paid with a photo canvas, so some are taps for the reward. The readout shows T56 answers beside Firebase's first user source for the same installs. On a tagged install the tag wins and a disagreeing answer is noise; on an organic or direct install T56's answer is the channel (O1).
- **Clip caveat (outside voice #2):** feed posts carry no link, and TikTok, Instagram and YouTube bio links often open in in-app browsers that drop Play's referrer, so most clip-driven installs arrive as organic; they're read through O1's organic bucket and T56's "video app" answer.
- **Clips (C1):** each clip platform's bio link is a T55 channel link (`utm_source` = `tiktok` | `instagram` | `youtube`), and the readout has a row for player shares of #154's feed cut (`share_completed` with `cut` = `feed`), so "a hit" can be checked against installs.
- No new attribution event (T46 eng O-E5 stands).
- Effort: human ~1d / CC ~45 min.

**T56: "Where did you find us?"**
- **Where (C3):** a small inline row on the completion screen of a fresh install's first finished picture (founder: "Something small, like please tell us where you found us and receive 1 free photo"). Not a sheet and not a return interruption: the one-interruption-per-return order and the reminder ask (design-system.md:506-511) are unchanged. Exact look and copy in `/plan-design-review`.
- **Reward (C3):** answering any of the five choices adds 1 photo canvas to the inventory, once, through the existing `Inventory.earnOnce(PHOTO_CANVAS, 1, "where-found")` (`Inventory.kt:145`: the earn id and the count go in one write, so it never pays twice and never loses the canvas; outside voice #8). Taps after the first are ignored. A failed inventory write can lose the canvas (eng outside voice #5). Skip, or ignoring the row, earns nothing. VIP has no inventory (design-system.md:517), so a VIP player sees the question without the reward line.
- **Who is asked:** every fresh install (T46's `NewPlayer` rule), at its first finished picture, and only when analytics consent is granted. With consent denied the answer would have nowhere to go, so the player is never interrupted for it. Installs with a referrer are asked too: the app keeps no referrer fact for the check (T50 deletes the raw string), and the answer cross-checks Firebase's first user source.
- **What:** one optional, one-tap question: community, video app, friend's share, Play search, other, skip. Asked once. Back, home or the app closing counts as skip. An install that never finishes a picture is never asked.
- Logged as one consented event (App interactions). It covers installs whose source Firebase can't name: a link copied by hand, a referrer the feed apps drop, direct.
- Copy in strings.xml for all 11 languages; TalkBack labels; the privacy policy and Data safety name it.
- **Asked once, enforced (CEO §2, eng R1):** the "asked" flag is written before the question shows through `CheckedStateStore.trySave`, implemented only by first_run's PrefsStore with `commit()`'s result (eng R1, D4); if the write fails the question isn't shown and a non-fatal is reported. A failed reward write is reported by the inventory store itself (eng outside voice #5).
- **Before promotion (CEO §3, the Constraints' C9 rule):** the founder re-saves Data safety (App interactions wording names the answer) and republishes the privacy policy before T56's build is promoted.
- **Eng outside-voice corrections (applied as required implementation of R1, R2, C3, §2, Scope #1/#2):**
  - The completion ViewModel keeps a per-`ref` "celebration done" flag (set at the end of the celebration effect, or at once when `sequence` is false), so a rotation mid-celebration (`CompletionScreen.kt:305-307`, `markPlayed` runs first) still shows the row (#1).
  - Two write moments on their own path, disk first then memory (#4, #7): `decided` at the first fresh finish through `trySave` (false: no row, ever, non-fatal); then best-effort `shown`, `answered`, `skipped`. Leaving mid-celebration stays `decided`.
  - Reward: VIP answers never call `earnOnce` (Inventory KDoc, `Inventory.kt:118-119`) (#10). A failed inventory write is reported by the store itself and can lose the canvas; T56 doesn't promise otherwise (#5, corrects the CEO §4 wording).
  - Analytics: one `where_found` event only on a tap (an answer or the skip button); leaving logs nothing. O2's T57 segment means `answer != skip` (#9).
  - Naming: the gate is `firstRunAsk`, never "fresh" (CompletionScreen's `fresh` already means a just-finished canvas) (#11). The `commit()` runs on the main thread, as T46's `gemPlaced` does (#12).
  - Test seams: test-only resets for the `first_run` state and a consent override, so the R2 connected test is deterministic; the ViewModel reads `firstRun` at call time (#2).
- **Timing (eng R2):** the row appears only after the finish celebration has cleared and Share shows; leaving before then counts as skip. `FinishCelebrationTest` gains a first-run, consented case asserting the unchanged sequence and the row not composed until the text has faded in (`text` at 1f), plus a reduced-motion case and a rotation-mid-celebration case (eng outside voice #1, #3).
- Visual design: specified in "Design Review (Phase 5) → T56 spec" (D3-D12) (the only new UI this round), which treats T56 and the reminder ask as two asks in one first session (outside voice #10).
- Effort: human ~1d / CC ~1 h.

**T57: Photo-first store listing (gated)**
- Gate: after production opens, photo canvases started, then finished, by strangers (T55 readout). **Bar (C4):** opens when the photo finish rate (photo canvases finished ÷ started) reaches **70% of the bundled finish rate at the same canvas size** (Quick, Medium, Big), pooled by the size mix of bundled starts, over at least 100 consented photo starts by players who never answered T56 (O2). Founder and test devices aren't excluded: at 100+ starts one device is noise, and the issue says so. Caveats written into the issue (outside voice #15): the photo colors choice also changes difficulty, and starter-size finishes have no photo match, so they're outside the comparison. If it stays closed, the listing keeps its current lead.
- **Reward bias (O2):** a spent reward canvas can't be told apart (`Inventory.kt:75,194`), so both the photo and the bundled cohorts leave out every player who answered T56 (a Firebase segment on T56's answer event). That removes the reward canvas and its nudge from the gate.
- **Not enough data:** if photo canvases started by consented players stay under T46's floor of 100, the gate stays closed and is re-read every 30 days until the count is reached. Each read is written into the issue.
- Scope when open: a Play store listing experiment on the default listing (en-US) plus up to four localized listings, the ones with the most store visits in Play Console (Play allows up to five languages per localized experiment). The variant's short description and first two screenshots lead with "your photo as gem art" (`tools/store/listing/<locale>/`). The other locales stay as they are until the experiment ends. Then: the variant wins → every locale switches; it loses → nothing changes. No result after 30 days → the current listing stays and the experiment is stopped.
- Effort: human ~2d / CC ~1 h (copy, screenshot captures through the existing per-language capture tooling).
