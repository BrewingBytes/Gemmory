Note on count: you asked for 40 ideas with at least 5 per mood across 9 moods. The founder then replaced the Privacy mood with "Your own photos" (at least 8). This file has **52 ideas: 5 per mood for eight moods (A-H), 10 in the "Your own photos" mood (I), plus own-photo ideas F6 and G6 woven into funny and seasonal**.

Abbreviations: **GP** = real gameplay capture (emulator), **CLIP** = Clips-screen 9:16 sparkle time-lapse, **AI-V** = AI video, **AI-I** = AI image. TT = TikTok, Reels = Instagram Reels, YTS = YouTube Shorts, GAC = Google App campaigns. Effort S/M/L.

## 1. Research

### Sources and what they say (and where evidence is thin)

1. **Genre creative trends.** SocialPeta's July 2026 report tracked a diamond-coloring game, Colorever, at about 6,800 deduplicated App Store creatives in a month (rank 11 on the global iOS game ad chart). The same report says tailoring creatives per market and reward-led hooks help. https://socialpeta.com/en/blog/top-mobile-game-ad-creatives-and-advertising-trends-in-july-2026
2. **Volume and hook fatigue.** Reforged Labs / SocialPeta: the average game advertiser ran about 123 active creatives per month in 2025. New hooks win early, then competitors copy them and performance normalizes. Vertical video remains the default format, and AI visuals work as attention hooks. Calm early-game gameplay is used by some advertisers instead of fail mechanics. https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/socialpeta-and-reforged-labs-mobile and https://mobidictum.com/creative-trends-report-2025/
3. **Native-looking and "real-life intro" formats.** Some titles open with a real-life scene or an ad that looks like organic Reels/TikTok content, then reveal the game (same SocialPeta / Reforged Labs coverage above). Also see AppMagic on UA trends: https://appmagic.rocks/blog/five-ua-trends
4. **Puzzle and lifestyle social proof.** AppsFlyer's creative optimization report says a social-proof emphasis in puzzle and lifestyle games lifted video performance (headline figure: 69%). I only saw the headline, not the method, so treat it as directional. https://www.appsflyer.com/resources/reports/creative-optimization-report/
5. **Diamond painting community.** Diamond painting spread on TikTok as a soothing, ASMR-adjacent craft (2020 Hypebae piece). https://hypebae.com/2020/11/diamond-painting-what-is-trend-tiktok-social-media-art-craft-rhinestones-designs-bts-asmr-quarantine-home-how-to-where-buy . Creators post under #diamondpaintingasmr on Lemon8 (dot-by-dot calm). A Philippines-based creator is reported with 275k+ TikTok followers and a 20M-view video mixing ASMR, unboxing and storytime (https://smstationery.com.ph/blogs/news/diamond-painting-a-sparkling-art-form-hobby-from-an-artist-s-view). Sound-focused craft ASMR travels: LEGO ASMR reached about 25.9M TikTok views (https://www.brickfanatics.com/lego-bricks-create-calming-asmr-videos). **Gap:** I found no hard numbers for "drill sound" or "reveal" videos specifically. Treat the ASMR/reveal angle as a hypothesis to test, not a fact.
6. **Policy pitfalls.** The UK ASA banned several mobile game ads (King, Scopely, Omnilojo, Vistrex) for misleading gameplay, and found that a "Not actual gameplay" disclaimer did not cure it. One ruling concerned Google ads. https://mobilegamer.biz/asa-bans-misleading-mobile-game-ads-from-king-scopely-dark-war-survival-publisher-and-more/ and https://www.asa.org.uk/rulings/scopely-inc.html . The ASA is stepping up monitoring (https://mobilegamer.biz/fake-mobile-game-ads-uk-regulator-steps-up-monitoring-while-us-agencies-stay-hands-off/). AppLovin prohibits ads that imitate system notifications or include fake interactive elements (https://legal.applovin.com/?p=1287). **Gap:** I did not retrieve the Meta, Google or TikTok policy text itself. Before launch, read each platform's current "misleading ads" and "app promotion" policy once.

### What this means for Gemmory

- **Show the real thing.** Our gameplay is calm and the payoff (sparkle, reveal) is real, so we can use real capture without any "fail" bait. This is both our strongest and our safest angle.
- **AI video must be framed as mood, never as gameplay.** Use AI-V/AI-I only for the "life around the app" (hands, a lamp, a pet, a gift), then cut to real GP/CLIP. Any shot that looks like app UI must be a real capture.
- **Hooks fade, so rotate.** Plan 4-6 hook variants per winning concept, and localize the on-screen text for the 11 languages once a concept wins.
- **Real-life intro then reveal** is a proven pattern, and it suits the cozy, gift and privacy ideas.
- **Hook patterns to reuse (first 1-3 seconds):** (a) the mid-fill sparkle close-up (satisfying frame first), (b) a before/after split (photo then gem canvas), (c) a bold question or relatable line, (d) a time-lapse already in motion at frame 0, (e) a number or time promise ("1 hour, one picture").
- **Lengths:** 9-20s for TT/Reels/YTS (hook, payoff, CTA), 15-30s for GAC. Vertical 9:16 everywhere, plus a 1:1 and 16:9 cut for GAC if possible. Captions always on (most views are muted, except ASMR ideas, which are sound-led, so put captions on those too).

### Policy guardrails used in every idea

- No fake UI, fake notifications, fake "fail" levels, or screens the app lacks. No "real diamonds / win money" claims. The word "diamond" always sits next to "painting" or "gems".
- Final 2-3 seconds of each ad is a real app screen or real CLIP output. No "not actual gameplay" disclaimers needed because nothing is faked.
- No celebrity, brand or copyrighted-character lookalikes. Masterpiece ideas use public-domain paintings the app ships (Van Gogh, Monet, Hokusai, Klimt are all public domain; use only pictures actually in the app).
- Adults only. No child actors or kid-directed language. Use stock-free, original or AI people only if clearly non-real (no real-person likeness). For AI people, check each platform's AI-content label rule (TikTok and Meta ask you to label realistic AI content).
- "Sensual" = light, gems, texture, ASMR, self-care. No bodies, no suggestive poses.
- Free-with-ads / VIP: do not promise "no ads" for the free tier. Do not claim features that are not live (iOS is not out; say "Android" or "Google Play").
- **AI labelling (EU AI Act Art. 50, in force since 2026-08-02, plus TikTok/Meta/Google rules):** any ad with AI video, AI images or an AI voice carries a small on-screen label ("AI-generated scenes" / "AI voice") while that part is on screen, and the platform's AI-content toggle is switched on at upload. Real gameplay and Clips output need no label. An own-photo ad whose starting photo is an AI image is labelled; a real photo we own is not.
- Own-photo ads: show only pets, places, drawings, people from behind, or the founder's own consented photo. Privacy is a trust detail, never the hook: at most a small end-card line "Photos stay on your phone." Do not say "100% secure" or "military grade".

### I. YOUR OWN PHOTOS (the core differentiator; backbone of the campaign)

Common spine: the hook is a phone photo the viewer recognises from their own camera roll, then the real transformation (GP of the photo becoming a canvas, then the CLIP sparkle time-lapse). Shared rules: use only pets, places, objects, drawings and people **from behind or too small to identify**, or the founder's own consented photo. Never show an identifiable stranger. ## 4. Simple test plan

**Budget:** about $600 total for the first round (adjust to what you have). Split:
- 50% Google App campaigns (UAC, Android installs): one campaign, all 10 creatives as assets (upload 9:16 plus 1:1/16:9 cuts where possible). Google rotates assets on its own, so judge by asset rating plus install-level reports.
- 30% TikTok: one ad group per mood cluster (satisfying / calm / privacy-gift-seasonal), 3-4 creatives each, broad targeting (adults 25-60, Android, your launch countries, English first).
- 20% Meta Reels: the 4 best-looking ideas (D1, I1, E4, C1), advantage+ placements, same targeting.

**Duration:** 7 days minimum, learning phase included. Do not edit budgets in the first 3 days. Minimum about 1,000 impressions per creative before judging the hook rate, and about 30-50 installs per variant before comparing CPI.

**Metrics, in order:**
1. **3-second view rate (thumbstop)** on TT/Meta: the hook test. Target above 30% as a first bar (a rough benchmark, not a source-backed number; use your own median after week one).
2. **CTR**: above about 1% is a first bar for TT/Reels.
3. **Cost per install (CPI)**: the decision metric. Cut the worst third; scale the best third by 2x.
4. **Quality check:** install to first picture finished (an existing Gemmory event if you track it), and D1 retention, because cheap installs that never place a gem are worthless. Note analytics are consented-only and floors apply (see `docs/reading-first-30-days.md`).

**Iteration rule:** for each winner, make 4-5 new hook variants (new first 2 seconds, same body), then localize the on-screen text for the top markets (de, es, pt-BR, fr, it, ja first). Replace losers weekly because hooks fade (see sources 2).

**Before any spend:** read the current Meta, Google and TikTok policy pages for misleading ads and AI-content labels (not retrieved in this research), and check that every shown picture, event (Advent, Christmas Week) and offline claim is true in the live build on the day the ad runs.
