# Ad production, round 1 (6 ads)

All 9:16, 15-20 s, captions on. Every ad ends on real app footage (gameplay or the Clips time-lapse) and an end card:
**"Gemmory — free on Google Play"**. Photos are CC0 from Wikimedia Commons (`photos/SOURCES.md`).

Shot sources: **AI** = AI video scene (ChatGPT/Sora), always with the on-screen label "AI-generated scenes" while it
plays and the platform's AI toggle at upload (EU AI Act Art. 50). **GP** = gameplay screen recording from the emulator.
**CLIP** = the app's sparkle time-lapse from the Clips screen. **PHOTO** = the CC0 photo itself, full screen.

Every AI prompt ends with: *"No text, no logos, no recognizable people or faces, no brands. Vertical 9:16, 5 seconds,
soft natural light, cinematic, calm."*

## What gets recorded on the emulator (one session)
1. Push the 6 photos into the emulator's gallery.
2. For each photo: pick it in the app (Medium or Big, so the detail holds), record the screen (GP).
3. Photo canvases: the Clips screen never renders a photo canvas (README → Making clips), so finish each photo canvas
   for real, fast, with the VIP Fill color tool (one tap per color, ~24 taps on Medium), then save the time-lapse the
   completion screen makes. It fills color by color, exactly what a player gets. Also record a stretch of normal
   one-gem taps for the GP shots.
4. Clips screen: render the sparkle time-lapse for the Rainbow Mandala (ad 4) and the Wreath Door (ad 5).
5. Share sheet with the sparkle video (ad 6).

Edit in CapCut: 1080×1920, captions in the app's fonts if possible, end card the same in all six.
