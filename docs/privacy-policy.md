# Gemmory privacy policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026

Gemmory: Photo Diamond Art ("Gemmory", "the app") is made by BrewingBytes ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app. Contact: andrei.serban@brewingbytes.com.

## The short version

- **Your photos never leave your phone.** We have no servers for your pictures, and the app never uploads them.
- The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect some device data to show and measure those ads, as described below.
- Purchases go through Google Play. We never see your payment details.
- There are no accounts, and we don't run any backend that stores your data.

## Your photos

When you make a photo canvas, you pick a photo with Android's photo picker. The app crops it, turns it into a gem grid and stores the result in the app's private storage on your phone. This all happens on the phone.

- Photos, grids and your progress are **not** included in Android cloud backups or device-to-device transfers.
- Face detection, which suggests a canvas size when a photo has faces in it, uses Google ML Kit's on-device model. **The photo is not sent anywhere.** ML Kit may send Google diagnostic and usage information about the face detection feature itself (for example device model, app version and performance metrics), never the image.
- You can delete a photo canvas from the gallery, delete all of them in Settings → Delete all photo canvases, or uninstall the app. Each of these removes the data from your phone.

## Sharing

When you tap Share, the app makes the time-lapse video (or a before/after picture) on your phone and hands it to the Android share sheet. It goes only where you choose to send it. The share text includes a Google Play link to the app.

## Ads

Gemmory shows ads from Google AdMob: a short video you choose to watch (to unlock a picture or get another photo canvas) and, occasionally, a full-screen ad after you finish a picture. Ads are never shown while you are placing gems.

To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

- your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
- approximate location derived from your IP address;
- information about how you interact with ads;
- diagnostic information.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before requesting ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Google's use of this data is described in Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and in "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services": https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## Purchases

VIP (weekly or yearly) and "Remove ads" are sold through Google Play. Google Play handles payment, and we never receive your card or payment details. The app asks Google Play which purchases your account holds, and keeps that answer on your phone so VIP works offline for a while. That record is not included in backups or device transfers. You can manage or cancel VIP in Google Play.

## Data we keep

We don't run servers that store your data. What the app keeps (your canvases, progress, settings, the daily photo canvas counter and your purchase status) stays on your phone. Settings such as sound and haptics, and your progress on the built-in pictures, may be included in your own Android backup, which Google stores for you.

## Children

Gemmory is meant for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. The app will be updated to describe any new kind of data use before it starts.
