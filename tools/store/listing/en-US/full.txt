Turn a photo of your own into a diamond painting, then fill it in gem by gem.

Gemmory is a calm color-by-number game for the evening. Pick a picture, tap a color, and set its gems one tap at a time until the whole canvas sparkles.

YOUR PHOTOS, IN GEMS
• Make a gem canvas from any photo on your phone: a pet, a trip, a garden.
• Choose a size: Quick for a short evening, Big for the detail.
• Your photos never leave your phone. Nothing is uploaded.

PICTURES TO PLAY
• A gallery of pictures to start: famous paintings, landscapes, flowers and mandalas.
• A new picture most days, plus Today's picture, free to start.
• Collections and weekly events with keepsake pictures to earn.

MADE FOR RELAXING
• Pinch to zoom, highlight the color you're on, find the next gem.
• Soft sounds and optional ambient music.
• A daily streak with days off and streak freezes, never pressure.
• An optional evening reminder, at the time you choose.

SHARE THE SPARKLE
• Every finished picture becomes a short sparkle video to share.

FREE TO PLAY
Gemmory is free with ads. Watch a short video to unlock a picture or get more fills. Ads never show on the canvas while you play.
Gemmory VIP (weekly or yearly subscription) unlocks every gallery picture, unlimited photo canvases, the brush and fill tools, no ads and no watermark on your videos. VIP renews automatically until you cancel in Google Play. You can also just remove ads with a one-time purchase.

Gemmory is made for adults who love diamond painting, cross-stitch and color by number.
