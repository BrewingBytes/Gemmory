Gemmory is a diamond painting game made from your own photos. Pick a picture, tap a color and set its gems one tap at a time, until the whole canvas sparkles.

WHY PLAYERS LOVE IT
• Unwind after a long day: no timer, no score, no rush.
• Finish a picture in one evening, or take a week over a big one.
• Watch your pet, your trip or your garden appear gem by gem.
• Share a short sparkle video of every picture you finish.
• Make a gift: turn a family photo into a picture and send the video to the people in it.

YOUR PHOTOS, IN GEMS
• Turn any photo on your phone into a gem canvas in seconds.
• Choose the size: Quick for a short evening, Medium, or Big for the detail.
• Choose the colors: Fewer for a calm canvas, More for a richer one.
• Your photos never leave your phone. Nothing is uploaded, ever.
• No account or sign-up needed: your pictures and progress stay on your phone.

HOW TO PLAY
• Every cell has a number, and every number has a color.
• Tap a color in the tray, then tap its cells to set the gems.
• A wrong-color tap does nothing, so you can't make a mistake.
• Pinch to zoom and drag to move around the canvas.
• The color you're on is highlighted, so its cells are easy to spot.
• Can't find the last few gems? One tap takes you to the next one.
• Your progress is saved with every gem. Close the app and pick up where you left off.
